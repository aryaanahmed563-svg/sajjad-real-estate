AI ACQUISITION ASSISTANT

Meet Wheel Jack

Wheel Jack is an AI acquisition assistant working with Sajjad's real-estate business. It can handle initial conversations, collect buyer and seller requirements, organize property information, help qualify opportunities and coordinate information between the people involved.


Wheel Jack is designed to assist the acquisition process — not replace human judgment. When authorization, negotiation, verification or an important decision is required, the matter can be brought to Sajjad or the appropriate human professional.
